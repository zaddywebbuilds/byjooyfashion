04 / The Craft

Nothing here
came off a roll.

Our adire is resist-dyed by hand. Cloth is tied, stitched or waxed before it ever meets dye, and where the resist holds the colour stays out. That is the whole trick, and it is why the same pattern never lands twice. Every other fabric we carry is chosen to the same standard.

  • 01
    Resist

    Tied, stitched or waxed by hand.

  • 02
    Dye

    Dipped in stages until the depth is right.

  • 03
    Open

    Resist cut away, pattern revealed.

  • 04
    Finish

    Cut, pressed and ready to wear.
